I have worked in healthcare since my early 20’s and have deep respect for allopathic medicine and believe that in this age we can integrate all the knowledge and healing modalities into our lives as we choose. It is never either or. However, allopathic medicine doesn’t get to the core of why something is happening to our minds and bodies, and our soul or psyche needs to be heard too if we are to live a truly vital life (and death), knowing and accepting our full selves at the deepest level. With my understanding of Anatomy, Physiology and disease processes and Shamanic practice, I bridge this gap, for people who are coming to alternative medicine from allopathic medicine, both in language and in energy. I understand that it can be a huge jump and many times goes against our upbringing and what we have been taught. I have 13 years of experience working shamanically with plant and fungi medicines and work with people of all ages, genders, abilities, cultural and religious beliefs.
