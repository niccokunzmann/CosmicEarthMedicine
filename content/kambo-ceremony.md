## What is Kambô?

Kambô is a sacred medicine ethically harvested from the big beautiful bright green, tree frog called the Phyllomedusa Bicolour. The Bicolour Tree Frog, native to the Amazon Rainforest, secretes what is known as Kambô, which is rich in peptides—molecules that act as communicators for our hormones and help regulate various bodily functions.

Our lymphatic system is incredibly responsive, and the effects of Kambô are usually felt within a minute. During a Kambô session, we drink up to 3 litres of water, which helps to support the medicine’s cleansing action and facilitate the purge (often through vomiting), further assisting the body in releasing toxins and stagnant energy.
